## MTV Rewind 1.1

**A native app, not a web page**
- **Just the video:** the site's banners, popups, counters and chat are gone.
- **Now Playing** card with the song, artist and channel.
- **Remote:** previous, play/pause, next, Save, Share, Skip and Full, with haptic clicks.
- **Channels**, **Time Machine** (decade and year), **Search** and **Saved** as native sheets.
- **Gestures:** swipe the picture for next/previous, tap to pause, double-tap for full screen.
- **Landscape** full-screen TV with a floating remote.
- **About** screen with credits and tips.

Still here from 1.0: Picture in Picture, background sound, lock screen controls, and the offline screen.

**Build fixes**
- Buttons work again: iOS's fullscreen video window no longer sits on top of the app.
- The video fills the screen after you turn the phone or tap Full.
- Coming back to the app always brings the video back from Picture in Picture.
- Uses the site's TV mode, so YouTube's own controls stay out of the picture.

**New colors**
- The site's MTV Rewind palette on black: royal blue panels, mustard, red, salmon, lilac and light blue, with chunky dark outlines.
- The play button looks like the site's Enter button: a pink-to-blue gradient with a yellow ring.
- New wordmark lettering and an icon redrawn in the same colors.

**New title**
- The app is now called **MTV Rewind** on your home screen and in the app.
- The title is now lavender with a brown 3D extrusion in heavy, condensed, slanted capitals, like an old VHS title card.

**Polish**
- Cleaner layout on an even spacing grid; the video sits in a rounded card.
- Clearer Now Playing with a smooth change between songs and placeholders while loading.
- Even, larger buttons and tiles, softer press animations, tidier sheets and loading screen.
- Better VoiceOver labels and support for larger text.
